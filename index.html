VIVA NOTE

How accuracy is maintained

Keep the sensor above the maximum water line, measure the empty-tank distance during calibration, reject impossible readings, and compare a few readings with a manual scale. If sensor data becomes invalid, the device should send an alert instead of making automatic pump decisions.
